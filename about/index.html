About this site

Solo Work Tools is run by Honest Little Apps, a small studio that builds iPhone apps. The apps on this site are EarnCheck, MoneyShield, ScopeBid, BookingSolved.

How a page gets written

  1. We start from one real question people type into a search box, not from a list of keywords.
  2. We build something useful on the page itself: a calculator, a checklist, a short playable demo, a printable, or a comparison we checked against the App Store on the day it was written.
  3. Health facts come only from NIH, CDC, NHS, Mayo Clinic, WHO or peer-reviewed sources, linked in the sentence where they are used.
  4. We describe our apps the way the App Store listing and screenshots show them. If a feature is not in the app, it is not on the page.
  5. Every page says who the app is not for.

What we do not do

  • No fake reviews, ratings, user counts or "as seen on" badges. Our apps are new and most have few ratings yet.
  • No cookies, no email capture, no pop-ups, no autoplay sound.
  • No paid placements. When we compare apps, ours is marked as ours.

Analytics

Pages use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies or personal identifiers. See privacy.
