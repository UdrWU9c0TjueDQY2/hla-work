Honest limits

Pro is a subscription.

After 3 free completed actions, bookings, reschedules and cancellations need Pro, monthly or yearly. Undo stays free. If December brings you 300 requests, that is the maths to do on day 12.

You still send.

Nothing goes out without your thumb. If you wanted a bot that answers clients at midnight, this is deliberately not that.

Apple Calendar only.

No Google Calendar, no Square, no Fresha. If your bookings live elsewhere, this is the wrong tool until they live in Apple Calendar.

No prices, no payments, no reminders to clients.

It books and replies. Deposits, no-show fees and client reminders are not in the listing.

Questions from the chair

Does BookingSolved reply to my clients for me?

No. The listing is clear: you send every reply yourself, and it never contacts clients or reads conversations in the background. It reads only the message you paste or share, prepares the booking against your Apple Calendar, and drafts a reply in Friendly, Professional or Concise tone for you to copy.

Do my clients have to use a new portal or link?

No. The listing says no new booking portal and no connected inbox; clients keep texting or messaging you the way they already do. That is the whole point for a salon that lives on Instagram DMs and texts.

What happens with “next Friday” or “4/5”?

Flagged, not guessed, per the release notes. You get an editable question to send back instead of a wrong booking.

Can it double-book me during the rush?

It checks your Calendar, re-checks right before saving, blocks a double tap from creating a duplicate, and uses each service’s duration and buffer to suggest open times. It will not know about appointments that are not in Apple Calendar, so that has to be your single source of truth.

What does it cost?

Your first 3 completed actions are free. Pro is a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription for unlimited bookings, reschedules and cancellations; the price shows in the app before you buy. Undo is always free.

Is my client data uploaded?

Requests and screenshots are processed on the device and not uploaded, per the listing; there is no account. Apple and RevenueCat get an anonymous ID and purchase status, never messages or Calendar data.
