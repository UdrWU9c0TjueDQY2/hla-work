Before you use the result

Why round down each day?

A 30-minute remainder on Monday and another on Tuesday cannot hold one continuous 60-minute appointment. Daily rounding respects that boundary.

What if I offer different services?

Run separate scenarios for different service lengths. An average can be misleading when actual appointments cannot be rearranged freely.

Can this book a client?

No. Use your calendar and confirm a real time with your client.
