Questions groomers ask

Do I need a booking portal for a dog grooming business?

A solo groomer can keep taking requests by text. BookingSolved prepares a calendar proposal and a reply the groomer reviews and sends.

Does BookingSolved read my messages?

It processes only a request you paste, a screenshot you choose, or something you share with it. You send each client reply yourself.

How should I handle “the usual”?

Ask which service the client wants. BookingSolved lets a groomer set the service name, duration, and prep or travel buffer.

Can two dogs fit in one slot?

Add the service time for both dogs and the cleanup or travel time. Check the whole window before offering the appointment.

Does BookingSolved take deposits?

Payments remain with your existing service. BookingSolved handles the calendar request and reply.
