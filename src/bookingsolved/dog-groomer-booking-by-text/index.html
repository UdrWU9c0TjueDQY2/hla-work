Honest limits

BookingSolved is a one-person tool built on Apple Calendar; it does not do Google Calendar, a team schedule, deposits, or a public booking link. It cannot read "the usual" without you, and it will not guess "next Friday". It never sends a message; every reply is yours to edit and send from the app the client used. Apple's guide to creating and editing events in Calendar is where the appointments end up, and the iPhone User Guide covers the Share Sheet the app uses to receive a message. We make the app, so read the listing yourself.

Questions groomers ask

Do I need a booking portal for a dog grooming business?

Not if your clients text you and keep texting you, which is most of them. A portal is one more place a booking can live. BookingSolved takes the other route: the client keeps texting, you keep Apple Calendar, and the app turns the message into a checked appointment and a reply you send yourself.

Does it read my messages?

Only what you hand it. Paste the text, choose a screenshot, or use the Share Sheet; the listing says it never reads conversations in the background and never contacts clients. There is no connected inbox.

What about repeat dogs and "the usual"?

Service presets hold the name, duration and prep or travel buffer for each service; there are starter lists for groomers among others, all editable. "The usual" with no service named is flagged for you to pick, because guessing a two-hour full groom when they meant a bath is how afternoons collapse.

Can it handle two dogs in one slot?

It books the slot and the service you choose. If two dogs mean a longer slot, set a service preset for that (Two-dog groom, 2.5 h) rather than hoping the buffer absorbs it.

Does it take deposits or payments?

No. No prices are stored and no money moves. If you take deposits, that stays wherever you take them now.

Is it free?
