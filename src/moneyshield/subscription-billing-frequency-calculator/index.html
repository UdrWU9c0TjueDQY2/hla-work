A worked example

Example: 10 every calendar month annualizes to 120. At 10 every four weeks, the 52-week convention gives 13 charges, or 130. That is 10 more for the same displayed price.

Watch $10 monthly versus every four weeks (12 seconds)

At $10 per charge, twelve monthly payments total $120. Billing every four weeks gives thirteen charges per 52 weeks, or $130. The calculator annualizes a recurring price; actual charge dates follow the service’s renewal terms.

Compare like with like

Use the same currency and include the same taxes or fees in both prices. The calculator does not convert currencies.

Annualized is not a bill calendar

Weekly estimates use 52 weeks; every-two-week estimates use 26 charges; four-weekly uses 13. An actual date range can contain a different number of charge dates.

Check the renewal terms

Trials, introductory prices, prorating and scheduled increases can change the next bill. Read the service's current terms before acting.

Before you use the result

Is a monthly equivalent what I will pay each month?

No. It spreads an annualized total over 12 months for comparison. Your actual charge schedule is unchanged.

Why not use four weeks per month?

Twelve four-week periods cover 48 weeks. A 52-week convention has thirteen four-week periods.

Does this cancel a subscription?

No. Nothing connects to a bank, billing account or subscription service.
