Before you compare

Why does annual cost stay flat?

This model treats the annual price as a single nonrefundable upfront payment. Stopping use after a few months does not change that payment. If your provider offers prorated refunds, this model does not include them.

Is the break-even month a recommendation?

No. It is a price comparison using your inputs. It does not measure usefulness, affordability, cancellation terms, or whether you will still want the service.

Does this save my prices?

No. Values stay in page memory and reset on refresh. This page has no tracking scripts or account requirement. Standard hosting request logs may still apply.

Comparing a weekly or four-weekly price?

First put each price on the same time scale. The subscription billing-frequency calculator shows annualized and monthly-equivalent costs, including the difference between every four weeks and every calendar month.
