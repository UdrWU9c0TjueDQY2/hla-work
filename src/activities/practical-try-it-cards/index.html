Independent companion activity · BookingSolved: Client Booking

Find the truly open start time

Try this independent scheduling exercise with fictional appointments. A service finishes at 10:00. Packing takes 10 minutes, travel takes 15 minutes, and the next setup takes 5 minutes. Write the four times in order: 10:00, 10:10, 10:25, 10:30. The earliest start under these assumptions is 10:30, not 10:00. Now change travel to 25 minutes and repeat: the start becomes 10:40. Keep service duration separate from the gap around it. BookingSolved offers service and buffer review against Apple Calendar for ongoing booking work; this paper exercise does not create an appointment or transfer into the app.

Independent companion activity · EarnCheck: Shift & Pay Tracker

Reconcile one fictional shift

Use a fictional shift from 09:00 to 17:30 with a 30-minute unpaid break and a flat example rate of 20 units per hour. Elapsed time is 8.5 hours. Subtract 0.5 hours to get 8 paid hours, then multiply: 8 × 20 = 160 units of estimated gross pay. Label every assumption beside the result. This example includes no overtime, premiums, taxes or deductions and makes no claim about legal entitlement. If a statement shows 7.5 hours, circle the half-hour difference for review rather than declaring missing pay. EarnCheck supports ongoing shift records and gross-pay comparisons; this standalone arithmetic does not transfer into the app.

Independent companion activity · ScopeBid: Estimate & Invoice

Calculate both percentages for one quote

Use a fictional job with direct costs of 800 units and a customer price of 1,000 units. Subtract cost from price: estimated gross profit is 200 units. For markup, divide 200 by 800 to get 25%. For gross margin, divide 200 by 1,000 to get 20%. Write the denominators beside each percentage so the difference stays visible. These values exclude overhead, tax and other business factors; they are not take-home profit or a pricing recommendation. ScopeBid displays markup and gross margin in its estimate workflow. This independent exercise explains the distinction without creating or transferring a quote.

Independent companion activity · MoneyShield: Sub Tracker

Put three fictional subscriptions on one scale

Use three fictional services in the same example currency: 8 units monthly, 30 units quarterly and 96 units yearly. Their estimated annual costs are 8 × 12 = 96, 30 × 4 = 120 and 96 respectively, for 312 units total. Write the original billing period beside every converted number. This comparison assumes unchanged prices and a full year; it is not a recommendation to cancel anything or a forecast of realized savings. MoneyShield supports recurring-cost comparisons and review decisions using the records entered. This standalone arithmetic does not connect to accounts, verify charges or transfer data into the app.
