The average hides a component

A fictional shift shows four hours at 20 plus a separate addition of 20, producing 100 gross. Someone divides 100 by four and labels 25 the base rate. Which statement preserves the supplied component information correctly?

Show the worked answer

C. The blended gross per hour is 25, while the stated base rate is 20

The total divided by hours gives a blended figure of 25, but the base component remains four hours at 20. Separating those measures prevents the additional payment from silently redefining the recorded base rate.
