Privacy

Short version: this site does not collect personal data.

  • No cookies. Nothing is stored in your browser except, on a few tool pages, your own inputs in local storage so the tool remembers them. That never leaves your device.
  • No accounts, no email capture, no forms that send anything to us.
  • Analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or fingerprinting and reports only aggregate page views.
  • Links to the App Store carry a campaign tag so we can tell which page a download came from. Apple's own privacy policy applies once you are on the App Store.
  • Fonts and images are served from this site, not from a third-party font service.

The apps themselves have their own privacy labels on the App Store, listed on each app's store page.
