Privacy

This site sets no cookies and collects no personal data.

Updated November 2026. Solo Work Tools is a static website. It does not use cookies, does not have accounts, forms, comments or newsletters, and does not store anything you type into its calculators or tools: those run entirely in your browser and nothing is sent to us.

Analytics

We may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-less and does not fingerprint visitors or track you across sites. It reports aggregate page views to us. Cloudflare's own description is at cloudflare.com/web-analytics.

Links to the App Store

Download links go to Apple's App Store. Apple's privacy policy applies there, and each app's own privacy details are listed on its App Store page. Our links carry a campaign tag so we can see which page a download came from; it contains no personal information.

Fonts and images

Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third-party font services.
