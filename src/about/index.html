About Solo Work Tools

A topic site from Honest Little Apps, a small studio that makes simple iPhone apps.

We make small, single-purpose iPhone apps and write the pages on this site ourselves. Each page is built around one useful thing: a calculator, a playable demo, a printable sheet, a comparison we checked against the App Store listings that day, or a question answered plainly with sources.

How pages are written and checked

  • We describe app features only as the App Store listing and screenshots show them. All of our apps are free to download.
  • Facts carry their source and date in the sentence. Health pages cite NIH, CDC, NHS, Mayo Clinic, WHO or peer-reviewed work, and they are general information, not medical advice.
  • We do not invent reviews, user counts, awards or press mentions, and we do not run countdown timers or fake scarcity.
  • Competitors, where we compare them, are described fairly from their own listings, and our own app is always disclosed as ours.
