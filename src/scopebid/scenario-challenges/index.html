Test fictional estimates for missing costs, percentage mistakes, scope ambiguity and payment-record discrepancies.

Choose a response. See the reasoning.

Read the facts, pick the answer that best fits the stated goal, then open the explanation. Start with five or browse the full set. There is no timer or personal assessment; the useful part is understanding the choice.

These are original web companion exercises. They are independent of the app; answers and progress do not transfer. All people and records in the scenarios are fictional. All financial arithmetic uses the assumptions stated in each example. It does not establish wages owed, legal rights or actual savings.
