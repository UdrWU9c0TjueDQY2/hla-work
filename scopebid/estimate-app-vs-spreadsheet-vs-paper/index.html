Which estimating method fits a one-person trade?

Short answer: paper pads work until you need to find what you charged last time; spreadsheets work until a customer wants to sign on site or the quote has to become an invoice; an estimate app earns its place when you quote more than a handful of jobs a month, reuse pricing, and want to see margin before you send. Full accounting software is the right pick when you have staff and a bookkeeper. Click through:

Do you send more than four quotes a month?

Below that, the tool matters less than the habit.

Do customers ever ask for a change mid-job, or an invoice from the quote?

That is where paper gets retyped.

Do you price new jobs by remembering what a similar past job cost?

If yes, that memory is your most valuable business asset and it is in your head.

Do you have employees or a bookkeeper who needs the numbers?

Payroll and ledgers belong in accounting software, not an estimate app.

Do you want to see gross margin before the quote goes out?

Spreadsheets can do this if you build it; most people never do.

Do you quote on site, sometimes with no signal?

Cloud tools and shared spreadsheets go quiet in basements and barns.

A carbonless pad, plus one habit

Under four quotes a month, few changes, no invoicing from the quote: a numbered carbonless estimate pad is fine. The one habit: photograph every sent page so you can find last year's price. ScopeBid is not the answer here.

A spreadsheet template

You quote regularly but only care about the customer price. A one-tab template with line items, tax and a PDF export covers it. Keep a second tab of past jobs by hand. If that tab ever gets painful, the next step is an estimate app.

Accounting software with estimating built in

Staff or a bookkeeper means the ledger is the centre of the business. Use the estimating module of whatever accounting package your bookkeeper already runs, so quotes, invoices and payroll live together. An estimate app would be a second system.

An estimate app, free tier

Few quotes, but changes and invoices happen. An app that turns an approved quote into an invoice without retyping saves the exact evening you lose today. ScopeBid's first three quotes are free with full PDFs, which may be all you need for a while.

Either: app if you reuse pricing, spreadsheet if you don't

At the desk with signal, a well-built spreadsheet can show margin. The app wins only if you want the last similar job's real cost next to the new quote, change orders with their own margin, and quote-to-invoice in one step.

How a quote goes in ScopeBid

Who should pick something else

Crews and companies

Once there is payroll, several people quoting, or a bookkeeper, a job-management or accounting platform is the centre and an estimate app becomes a second system.

Trades that bill hourly after the fact

If you rarely quote ahead and mostly invoice time, an invoicing-first tool fits better than an estimate-first one.

Anyone who needs online payment links

ScopeBid records payments; it does not collect them. If "pay now" buttons matter, look elsewhere.

Questions solo trades ask

Is a spreadsheet good enough for contractor estimates?

For a few jobs a month with stable pricing, yes, if you also keep a PDF export habit and never edit the sent copy. It breaks when you need the last similar job's real cost, when the customer wants to sign on site, or when the quote has to become an invoice without retyping.

What should every contractor estimate include?

Scope in plain words, line items with quantities and units, labour and materials separated, trip charge or minimum, tax shown separately, deposit and payment stages, a validity date, and your terms. The checklist at the top of this page is that list.

Does ScopeBid process payments?

No. It records deposits and partial payments received by cash, cheque, e-transfer, bank transfer or a card terminal and tracks the balance. It does not process cards or move money, and it is not accounting or tax software.

Is ScopeBid free?

The first three quotes are free with full PDFs, and everything you create stays viewable and exportable whether or not you subscribe. ScopeBid Pro, a monthly or annual subscription, adds unlimited quoting plus the full Business Memory, job-costing and invoicing workflow.

Does it work with no signal?

Yes. Quoting, PDFs, invoices and records work offline, there is no account, and there is no cloud copy of your business data. Back up from Settings when you want one.
